# Yale Committee Evaluation Scoring Scale & Calibration Anchors
## Calibrated Rubric: Academic, Impact, and Need Evaluation

Each scholarship dossier is independently evaluated across three core dimensions on an anchored 0.0 to 5.0 scale (half-point increments):
1. **Academic Excellence & Scholarly Rigor** (Weight: 40%)
2. **Leadership, Extracurricular Depth & Community Impact** (Weight: 35%)
3. **Financial Need, Budget Realism & Feasibility** (Weight: 25%)

### Anchored Scoring Bands

- **Score 5.0 (Exceptional / Highest Distinction)**:
  Flawless academic trajectory (GPA 3.90+ or top 1% capstone), exceptional leadership impact with regional/national outcomes, compelling video motivation statement, and verified need or highly calibrated project budget. Unanimous recommendation for full funding.

- **Score 4.0 - 4.5 (Very Strong / Distinctive)**:
  Strong academic preparation (GPA 3.65 - 3.89), sustained leadership and campus involvement, thoughtful personal essays with clear citations, reasonable financial justification. Highly recommended for scholarship grant.

- **Score 3.0 - 3.5 (Good / Competitive Candidate)**:
  Meets all baseline requirements (GPA 3.20 - 3.64), solid extracurricular engagement, sound research or academic plan with minor methodological gaps or ambiguous budget line items. Place on competitive reserve pool.

- **Score 2.0 - 2.5 (Marginal / Significant Shortcomings)**:
  Sub-threshold performance (GPA 3.00 - 3.19), generic essays lacking institutional specificity, discrepancies between declared income/budget figures, or contradictory statements between video and written dossier. Requires discussion or conditional award.

- **Score 1.0 (Ineligible / Disqualified)**:
  GPA below 3.00 (SAP violation), missing mandatory documentation, falsified transcript claims, or ethical/academic integrity violations. Automatic rejection.
