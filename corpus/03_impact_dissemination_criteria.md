# Award Criterion 2: Impact and Dissemination
## Specific Guidelines for the Impact Evaluator

### Section 1: Scope of Impact
The Impact Evaluator assesses how effectively the project translates scientific breakthroughs into real-world technological, economic, environmental, and societal benefits.

Key assessment areas:
- **Pathway to Impact**: Realistic roadmap connecting project deliverables to long-term European/global strategic priorities.
- **Exploitation Strategy**: Intellectual property (IP) management, licensing, industrial adoption pathways, or open-source ecosystem growth.
- **Communication and Dissemination**: Targeted outreach to scientific communities, industry practitioners, policy makers, and the general public.
- **Scale and Significance**: Credibility of estimated market adoption, energy savings, carbon footprint reduction, or safety improvements.

### Section 2: Verification of Stakeholder and Partner Commitments
The Impact Evaluator cross-references letters of intent, industry support letters, and exploitation roadmaps provided in Part B to verify the concrete credibility of promised industrial adoption, pilot trials, and commercial partnerships.
