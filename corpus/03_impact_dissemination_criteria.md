# Yale Leadership, Community Impact & Global Engagement
## Evaluator Guideline 2: Public Purpose, Community Leadership & Cultural Contribution

### Section 1: Character & Service to Society
In line with Yale’s mission to educate leaders who serve all sectors of society, the Impact Reviewer assesses:
1. **Demonstrated Leadership**: Evidence of initiative, sustained commitment, resilience, and collaborative problem-solving within campus organizations, public service initiatives, or research collectives.
2. **Community Impact**: Clear articulation of how the student's work benefits their broader community, field of inquiry, or society.
3. **Dissemination & Reach**: Feasibility of translating research, creative arts, or public policy proposals into tangible outcomes, peer-reviewed publications, open-access tools, or community workshops.

### Section 2: Narrative Coherence Across Materials
- Evaluators examine whether the student's personal statement, video motivation letter, and extracurricular record reinforce a coherent life trajectory.
- Authenticity check: Statements must reflect the applicant's genuine voice and lived experiences rather than formulaic templates.
- Any conflict between claims in the essay and the factual CV/transcript triggers a review flag.
