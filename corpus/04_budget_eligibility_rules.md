# Yale Financial Aid & Fellowship Budget Eligibility Rules
## Evaluator Guideline 3: Cost of Attendance, EFC, Student Share & Outside Scholarship Policies

### Section 1: Demonstrated Need Calculation (Deterministic Formula)
1. **Financial Need Formula**:
   $$\text{Demonstrated Need} = \text{Cost of Attendance (COA)} - \text{Expected Family Contribution (EFC)}$$
2. **Cost of Attendance Components**:
   - Billed Direct: Tuition, Housing, Food.
   - Unbilled Indirect: Books & Supplies, Personal Necessities, Travel Allowance.
3. **Expected Family Contribution (EFC)**:
   - **Parent Share**: Determined through comprehensive institutional formula assessing family income and assets.
   - **Student Share**: Fixed standard annual self-help expectation of strictly **$3,700** for term-time and summer earnings.
4. **Income Tiers**:
   - Families earning under $75,000 with typical assets qualify for **Zero Parent Share** (full tuition, housing, food, travel, and hospitalization coverage).
   - Families earning between $75,000 and $200,000 qualify for sliding-scale need-based tuition and living grants.

### Section 2: Outside Scholarships & Tech Purchase Policy
- **Outside Awards**: Outside scholarships first reduce or eliminate the $3,700 Student Share before reducing the Yale Scholarship.
- **Computer Purchase Allowance**: Students with outside awards exceeding their Student Share may utilize up to **$2,500** of excess funds as a one-time technology allowance for a computer purchase.
- **Non-Eligible Budget Items**: Luxury personal travel, non-academic equipment, discretionary investment funds, and duplicate federal allowances are strictly deducted.
